# Thread AI

> Thread AI builds AI orchestration infrastructure for enterprises. Its platform, Lemma, is a composable AI infrastructure and workflow-orchestration layer for deploying production-grade AI agents and workflows in regulated, mission-critical operations, with full audit trails, governance, and reliability controls. Thread AI is backed by Homebrew and Index Ventures.

## Platform

- [Lemma Platform](https://www.threadai.com/platform): Composable AI orchestration infrastructure and workflow builder — drag-and-drop builder, code-first SDK, and REST/gRPC/GraphQL API access, with human-in-the-loop, streaming/batch/interactive execution, and embeddable white-label components.
- [AI-Readiness Guide](https://www.threadai.com/guide-critical-production-ai): Framework for deploying production-ready AI in critical processes.
